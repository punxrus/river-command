# Story bible

Canon facts. The narrative-designer agent reads this first and adds to it.

## Setting
- **Rock Harbor**: fictional fishing and tourist town at the mouth of the Alder River, Pacific Northwest coast.
- **Rock Harbor Station**: Coast Guard small boat surf station. Two 47' Motor Lifeboats, one 29' RB-S.
- The campaign follows the river inland: Act 1 at the mouth, Act 2 upriver (barges, locks), Act 3 the basin.

## Tone
Grounded and understated. Competent, tired professionals. Drama from weather, time, and choices.

## Rules
- All people, vessels, and places are fictional.
- No real tribal names, symbols, or stories. Any Indigenous community is written as a respected partner,
  with no invented sacred material, and flagged for community review.
